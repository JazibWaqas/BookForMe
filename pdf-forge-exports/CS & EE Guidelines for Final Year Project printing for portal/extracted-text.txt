Guidelines for Final Year Project (FYP) Submission for Library Clearance 
 
Students must complete, sign in the approval form (available here) by the faculty / advisor before 
sending it for final printing. A copy of this form will be required for library clearance as well. 
Length  
The minimum length of FYP should be 12000 words (excluding table of contents and 
preliminary pages).  
Formatting 
• A4 paper 
• Use Latex for formatting papers with Computer Modern font 
• Graphs/Graphics should be color printed (if recommended by the advisor).  
• Use American English  
Printing 
• Single sided printing is essential.  
• The template having essential sections of the report can be downloaded here 
1. Computer Science (filename: CS_Kaavish_report_template final.pdf) 
2. Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE) - (filename: EE FYP report template 
final.pdf) – a zip file is also available for page by page editing and formatting (EE 
FYP Report Format with Pages.zip). 
• Printing can be done in house or through Universal Photostat (contact details mentioned 
below) according to following paper specifications. 
1. Black and White 80 grams  
2. Color printing 105 grams 
Binding 
• FYP must be hard bound in University Blue with Gold Lettering (see specification 
below) 
• The binding should be done through Madina Book Binding House (contact details 
mentioned below) 
• The format of spine is available here for downloading.  
• The golden logo file is available here for downloading (filename golden logo.png)  
Printing and binding costs will be borne by the students.   
Submission of Report and Library Clearance 
• For library clearance, students have to submit a hard bound copy and a soft copy with 
metadata here  https://habibuniversity.sharepoint.com/sites/Student/hira-metadata-form 

• Along with hard bound copy, the advisor / supervisor approval form should also be 
submitted.  
• Library will only accept the reports, which have been duly signed by respective faculty 
member and/or supervisor. 
• Library books have to be returned (as per due date) and all fines have to be paid.
